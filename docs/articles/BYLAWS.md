# Neighborhood DFAO — bylaws / knobs

**TEMPLATE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**

Knobs for a MESO neighborhood DFAO running alongside an HOA. Votes rewrite **this** room. They do not rewrite the mesh or the county’s deed records.

## Quorum

SHADOW: whoever closed a loop this cycle.  
HYBRID / ACTIVE: 30% of actives, or 5 people, whichever is larger. Overridable.

## Deliberation

Days, not hours. Common-area spend waits one cycle unless the light is actually out.

## Voting

Linear IT inside a MICRO crew. Conviction or quadratic at MESO if the room is big. IT decays ~5%/month if you vanish. A MICRO vote cannot rewrite a MACRO.

## What may bind (HYBRID)

- Which jobs get posted
- Confirmation rules
- Who operates a node
- Settle window (starting number ~30 days, not a law)
- Whether to hire a lawyer to *read* the declaration

## What may not bind until the declaration says so

- Assessments that run with the land
- Fines, liens, foreclosure
- Architectural control in the CC&Rs
- Dissolving the existing association

## Work

LocalFlow is the face. Nobody has to say XP. SignalFlow proposes ΔS. Both edges agree or it is a problem.

## Conduct

No vandalism. No targeting people or vehicles. That is a crime and it is entropy. You are trying to be the less-disordered option.

## Amendment of these knobs

Quorum of actives, recorded on the DAG. PLANETARY is not this neighborhood’s hobby.
