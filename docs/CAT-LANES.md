## Overlay, not a fourth school

4. Raw stays on the box or drops. The mesh sees the hash.

When they join they become an **issuer**. They sign the interval. You hold the record. Spite revoke is a vertex on the issuer. Other rooms may ignore that issuer. You do not invent a second chess number.
