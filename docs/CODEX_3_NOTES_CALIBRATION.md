## The claim, steel-manned

The eight domains are a **weighted vector**, not eight sermons. A mediation and a mowed lawn do not share a constant on day one. They share a graph that gets denser.

Every closed loop hangs evidence and a provisional ΔS on a vertex with causal parents. Next time a similar spousal fight, sibling fight, lawn, ride — the network has somewhere to look besides a vibe. SignalFlow + the edge model propose; they do not invent in a vacuum.

**Digital Autarky + ZKP:** edge intelligence unveils only what the equation needs. Nobody sits on the diary. No Big Brother. Detail without a panopticon is the whole privacy thesis.

**Landauer is the bridge, not a costume.** Information erased has a heat floor. Social, cognitive, intellectual events are information events. They can be scored as weighted if-then across domains. That is not “a joule for a quarrel” on day one. It is a claim that the same accounting can hold an information event and a thermodynamic event without an extractive FX desk between them.

**Convergence, not eternal subjectivity.** More like-cases, more surviving interpretations, less needle movement. Edge models will still read a scene differently. The bet is that a stack of similar scenes plus ZKPs plus no-friction outcomes produces a number stable enough to append to “this kind of event.” Calibration. The scientific method as currently practiced (isolated lab, privileged thermo) is itself an epistemic frame — not a reason to call everything outside the calorimeter a metaphor and stop.

**Human in the loop is a job.** DAG specialist / curator: organize the ledger, mark which slice of history should weight a new like-case, ingest more data (including purchased anonymized corpora, with eyes open that those corpora were born extractive). That work is entropy reduction. It mints XP. The graph feeding itself is the point. Incentivize pedantic detail until the next crop has a less stupid proxy.

**No extractive microtransaction at each hop.** Transactionality is the loop. The machine talks to its own history. Weighted consensus of how “this” was interpreted before, and whether that interpretation ended without pushback. That is the part other systems do not pay for, because their rent is the hop.

**The founder has no privileged key.** That is the trust model. Not a cult of method. A system built because he does not trust himself with a score. If 3.0 reintroduces a founder override, it has failed this note.
